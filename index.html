METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains how knowledge becomes visible business action

My Cabinet is not a list of links. Each treatment is designed to move from raw evidence to a structured decision that can be learned, repeated and measured.

D
Data

Raw signals, facts, interactions, operational inputs and business evidence.

I
Information

Data is structured into useful variables, contexts and decision-ready records.

I
Intelligence

Models, rules and AI interpret the information and connect it to the business problem.

I
Insights

The treatment exposes priorities, risks, opportunities and measurable management signals.

P
Personalisation Actions

The insight becomes a practical action: activate, prioritise, diagnose, convert, decide or measure.

KAI·ROI EQUATION · CUSTOMER EQUITY

The Cabinet feeds the KAI·ROI decision layer

The tools create structured operational evidence that can support implementation, ROI analysis, Customer Equity reasoning and defensible strategic decisions.

The formal KAI·ROI structure remains sovereign: individual tools do not redefine or reinterpret its variables unless explicitly designed to do so.
